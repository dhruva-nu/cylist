# Tidying the Atlas board

When asked to tidy the board:

1. List every card in "In progress" whose last activity is older than five days.
2. For each, add a comment asking the owner whether it is still being worked on.
3. Never move a card yourself; a person decides that.

Finish by saying how many cards you commented on.
